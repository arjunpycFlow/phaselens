# Code of Conduct

PhaseLens adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a private [security advisory](https://github.com/arjunpycFlow/phaselens/security/advisories/new) marked "Conduct". Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
