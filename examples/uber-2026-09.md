**Prompt:** *"Is Uber a buy, and at what price?"*

## Phase: 3 → 4 (Operating Leverage moving into Capital Return)

| Signal | Reading |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth, 3 years | $31.9B (2022) → $52.0B (2025), about 18% a year |
| Operating margin | about 13% in Q2 2026, still expanding |
| Cash | FCF $0.39B (2022) → $3.4B → $6.9B → $9.8B (2025) → more than $10B trailing twelve months; buybacks started in 2026 |

Profits and cash flow are compounding and cash has started going back to shareholders, so forward multiples and the reverse DCF are both valid.

## Moat: B

Two-sided network (208M monthly users, bookings +22% for four straight quarters). The main risk to the grade is robotaxi operators going direct to riders.

## Insider signal

The CEO bought about $10M of stock on the open market on September 15, 2026, at about $70.96.
